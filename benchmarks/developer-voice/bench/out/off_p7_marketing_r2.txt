**Introducing [Product]: reads that finish before you notice them**

Today we're shipping [Product], a distributed caching layer that sits between your services and your primary datastore. The headline: p99 read latency drops from 180ms to 12ms — a 15x reduction on the requests that used to hurt most. Not the median, which was never the problem. The tail. The one read in a hundred that stalled a page, tripped a timeout, or turned a clean dashboard into a support ticket.

Tail latency is where distributed systems quietly lose their reputation. A p50 of 8ms looks great in a status meeting, but users don't experience averages — they experience the slowest thing that has to happen before the screen renders. When a single page fans out to a dozen backend reads, a 180ms p99 stops being an edge case and becomes the common case: the more reads you make, the likelier you are to hit one. [Product] was built to flatten that curve, and the numbers below show what that looks like in production.
