The old path was straightforward and slow. Every read went to the primary datastore, so tail latency tracked whatever that datastore was doing under load: lock contention, cold pages, a noisy neighbor on the same shard. [CacheName] keeps hot keys in memory across a cluster of nodes and routes each read to the node that owns the key. Reads that hit the cache never touch the datastore. Reads that miss fall through, populate the cache, and stay fast for everyone who asks next.
